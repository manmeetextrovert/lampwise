Questions

Does Lampwise use AI?

No. It checks your prompt against a set of rules, asks questions from a written list, and fills in a template.

Where does my prompt go?

Nowhere. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing is uploaded or stored.

Which tools can I use the result with?

Any chatbot or writing tool that accepts text. The prompt is plain text.

Why only three questions?

Three is enough to fix the biggest gaps without slowing you down.
